The fabric

Woven for summer

Our lawn is woven from long-staple cotton, printed and embroidered in Lahore.

Each season we draw new motifs from the same archive that feeds our bridal — Mughal jaali, Kashmiri florals, the geometry of old havelis — then reduce them to a single thread of colour. The result is a suit that breathes in August and holds its shape for years.
