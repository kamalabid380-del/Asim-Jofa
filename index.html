The House

Couture with a memory

Asim Jofa began in 2009 with a single conviction: that an outfit should carry meaning long after the occasion has passed.

From our ateliers in Lahore, master artisans work each piece by hand — zardozi, dabka and pearl, set stitch by stitch over weeks and sometimes months. The result is not fashion in a season, but an heirloom in the making.
