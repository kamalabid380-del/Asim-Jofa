The craftsmanship

Set, never glued

Kundan is a setting without claws — gold is worked into ribbon-thin strips and pressed around each uncut stone.

Our goldsmiths learned the technique from their fathers. A single bridal set takes six to nine weeks and passes through eleven pairs of hands. Each piece is hallmarked, weighed in your presence, and delivered with a certificate of origin.
